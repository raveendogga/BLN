Details of Project Work 
M.E (Masters Degree Dissertation): Design of Current Mode Controlled 50 kHz Fly-Back 
 Regulator Power Supply for Travelling Wave Tube (TWT)   
Project Guide: Sri. G.S.N. Raju, Sc.G, Additional Director, LRDE, Ministry of Defence, Bangalore-93

Description: The objective of this project is to achieve optimal design to get improved performance and to realize in compact size, also this provides well-organized useful information for the design engineer who wants to understand the current mode-controlled power converter. A medium voltage source capable of maintaining constant current, mainly towards realize the scale down power supply, has been studied practically and implemented. The project was quite successful and the developed supply is used as filament supply in Radar applications. 

Ph.D Doctoral Thesis: An Improved Bi-directional Converter 
Thesis Supervisors: Prof. S. P. Singh & Dr. S. P Dubey, IIT Roorkee. 
Description: The battery backup system is very essential for several applications such as DC-UPS systems, automotive power management, telecommunications, and renewable energy application.  The back-up batteries have to be charged and discharged depending on the bus power demand. Therefore, the Bidirectional DC to DC (BDC) converters are very attractive for these battery backup systems. The BDC converter is required to transform the low battery voltage to a suitable and much higher DC link voltage However, the key challenge lies in providing an efficient and scaled own BDC converter with input and output voltage conversion for such applications where low voltage batteries are used. The objective of thesis work is to present a cost effective, scaled down and high efficiency BDC converter for efficient use of the energy storage system. Therefore, the thesis work is specifically concerned with developing an improved BDC converter for discharge /charge application using a low cost PWM IC’s controller, and to validate the performance experimentally. The thesis work presented an improved BDC converter using coupled inductor concept, which provides high efficiency with voltage diversity as compared to the conventional buck and boost derived BDC converter. Thesis work address the design, modelling, control, stability issues and further improvised version of the proposed BDC converter. The prototype of BDC converter is developed and the validated experimentally.
