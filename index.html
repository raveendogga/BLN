Profile Summary

Prof. B. L. Narasimharaju is a distinguished Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Warangal. With a profound career spanning over 24 years in research, teaching, and academic leadership, he has contributed extensively to the fields of Power Electronics and Renewable Energy Systems.

He holds a Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee (awarded in 2012) focusing on the development of highly efficient bidirectional power converters. As a senior academician, he has successfully driven large-scale government-funded research programs, mentored multiple doctoral scholars, and established international research collaborations. He also serves as an Adjunct Professor at SRM University AP and was formerly the Head of the Electrical Engineering Department at NIT Warangal.

Areas of Research Interest
Solar Electronics & Applications

Solar-powered LED lighting systems, PV fed water pumping systems, and solar-PV inverters for grid integration.

E-Mobility & Energy Storage

Electric vehicle propulsion drives, battery storage integrations, and bidirectional power converters for EV charging.

Magnetic Design & Modeling

High-frequency magnetic component design, converter modeling, stability analysis, and feedback control loop designs.

Advanced Power Conversion

Induction heating system optimization, soft-switching techniques (ZVS/ZCS), and grid-interactive converters.

Educational Qualifications

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D. in Power Electronics)
Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee (2009 - 2012)

Thesis Topic: An Improved Bi-Directional Converter

Supervisors: Prof. S. P. Singh & Dr. S. P. Dubey

Description: Focused on addressing the challenges of battery backup systems for DC-UPS, automotive power management, and renewable energy applications. Developed an improved Bidirectional DC-DC Converter (BDC) utilizing coupled-inductor topologies to transform low battery voltage to a high DC-link voltage with superior efficiency and high voltage diversity. Validated performance through experimental prototypes.

Supervisor: Sri. G.S.N. Raju, Sc.G, Additional Director, LRDE, DRDO, Bangalore

Description: Achieved optimal and compact design of current mode-controlled flyback regulators. The scaled-down power supply was successfully deployed as a filament supply in military Radar applications.
